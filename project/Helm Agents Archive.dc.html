Alethiometer · Archive
Rules
  • a, or right-click › Archive, archives the selected row and only that row: a session, a room, a group, or an Unsorted session. Archiving a session leaves its group.
  • Nothing that’s running or waiting for you can be archived, and neither can a room or group with members still running. Stop them first.
  • Archiving deletes the Hub agents and their clones. Messages and transcripts stay searchable. Unsorted local sessions are only hidden.
  • The confirm shows the tree as it will change: rows that will be archived are lit, and the rest are marked unchanged. The tree behind it dims the same way.
  • ⌘⏎ archives. ⌘⌫ is only for Archive anyway, the red force-archive on something not done. Esc cancels, and u undoes for 10 seconds.
  • Archived items leave the tree. They come back only through the tree filter /, and they open read-only. No restart, no unarchive.
  • ⌘K doesn’t archive and doesn’t list archived items.
