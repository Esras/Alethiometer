11Mac + iPhone · cross only through the services themselves, with receipts
No device link
The Mac and iPhone never talk to each other directly. Life comes in from a Proton Calendar Limited view link, which shows only busy times. Life goes out through Marvin's API into its Inbox, and the iPhone picks it up through Marvin's own sync. Replaces 10a's KDE Connect.
Receipts, then clear
A ~ line stays on the Mac until Marvin replies with the new task's ID. Only then is the line marked ✓ and cleared. If it fails, the line stays and says why. Nothing is ever deleted without that reply.
Work notes
A separate work Obsidian vault fits best, because Helm reads the files directly and no extra service sees them. Sync it only through storage your organization's BAA covers. Don't use Obsidian Sync or iCloud tied to your personal accounts.

10Work laptop sees the shape of life, not its content
In
Your personal calendar's free/busy feed is the only thing that comes in. It's a URL that only carries times, with no titles. Helm draws those as untitled Life blocks, and they count down just like meetings. "Leave by" is worked out on the laptop from a travel buffer you set per block.
Out
A ~ line in Notes goes to your phone over KDE Connect when you send, and is then deleted from the laptop. Pressing ↵ on a Life block pings the phone to open that time in its calendar. Helm stores no personal account logins.
Stays out
Personal event titles, task lists and notes are never stored on the laptop and never reach the work AI. Blue work-calendar holds like Lunch and Walk stay as they are, because they already belong to work.
10aNotes on the work laptop. Physio and Pickup are now untitled Life blocks, with a leave-by tick before Pickup. Physio is selected, and its popover shows everything the laptop knows. The dentist line now starts with ~ and goes to the phone.

9Notes and Reflect tabs · three kinds of todo
Your notebook glyphs
Do · square, blue · [] Research · circle, green, no outcome needed · () Delegated · triangle, red, with a name · ^ @name Note · dash · -
The glyph's shape carries the meaning, so the blue and green here can't be mistaken for the Personal and Jobs category bars.
Notes
A writing surface, not a todo list. Type freely; lines break out into suggested topics, people or apps (Marvin / Jira) in a margin, and one send drops them into Incoming. A delegated line can offer a Jira task, but doesn't have to become one. The paper notebook stays separate. Super+J captures from anywhere.
Reflect
Built to stop, so there's no day strip, search or assistant pane; only a small countdown pill when a meeting is near. The app nudges you into it at the end of the day (9d). Day · Week · Month · Quarter each write their own Obsidian note, and each level rolls up the one below. Carry forward works like bullet-journal migration: > tomorrow, < schedule, or × let go, with no guilt language.
9aNotes, rebuilt as a writing page. You just type, and prefixes become glyphs. A fixed "Goes to" margin quietly suggests a topic, person or app for each line, all left-aligned in one column. Tab on a line changes it. One send moves everything into Incoming, where the real triage happens.
