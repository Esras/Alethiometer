19Gmail in Incoming · todo + archive, trash, unsubscribe, labels
19aGmail rows. Triage (Confirm, Wrong topic, Dismiss) is unchanged. After a divider come the Gmail-only actions: Todo… (e), Trash (#), and, when the email has a one-click unsubscribe header, Unsubscribe (⇧U). Trash also dismisses the email. Unsubscribe sends the one-click request and then trashes it. Both leave the row struck through with u undo, like Dismiss, and each acts on one email at a time. The category chip is the Gmail label: they sync both ways, and the tag icon means it's linked. There's no plain Archive. Archiving happens only through a todo (19b), so nothing leaves the Gmail inbox without a follow-up. Newsletters stay in the normal list.
